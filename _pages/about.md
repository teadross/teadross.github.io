Research Interests
======  
My research interests mainly span two areas: network optimization, covering both backbone networks and data center networks, and blocking theory, including set-theoretic foundations for non-blocking networks and queueing-theoretic analysis for blocking networks.

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <h3>Data Center Networks</h3>
    <p>My work focuses on AI-driven network architectures that support diverse services such as unicast, multicast, and co-flow. I have designed optical-circuit-switching-based architectures and algorithms for efficient service deployment.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <h3>Backbone Networks</h3>
    <p>I study blocking theory for switching networks and next-generation switching elements. My work includes strictly non-blocking conditions, blocking probability models, and Optical Cross-Connect (OXC) designs.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <h3>Cloud-Edge Networks</h3>
    <p>I focus on survivability and resilience in MEC scenarios, including integer linear programming models and heuristic algorithms for access-point and link protection.</p>
  </div>
</div>
